2 Developer Logic — Calculated Expiry (Legacy Fallback)

Refreshes the token twice within 30 days of the standard x_refresh_token_expires_in date, then compares old vs. new expiry. If old_expiry == new_expiry, the token has hit its 5-year hard limit.
