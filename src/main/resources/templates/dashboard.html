To receive webhooks:

  1. Make sure your app is connected to QuickBooks
  2. Configure webhooks in developer.intuit.com
  3. Set webhook URL to your public endpoint: https://your-url/webhooks
  4. Create/update an entity in the QuickBooks UI or send a test webhook in the Intuit Developer Portal
  5. Watch webhooks will appear here
